Python Data Analysis Pandas & NumPy

DataCo Supply Chain Analysis

A data cleaning and exploratory analysis project based on a supply chain dataset containing 180,519 rows and 53 columns. The workflow covered dataset inspection, missing-value handling, data-type validation, and data preparation before analysis. Key business metrics were then calculated across sales, delivery performance, markets, and product categories. The analysis generated $36.78M in total sales, a $203.77 average sales value, and a 54.70% late delivery rate, with Europe identified as the top-performing market and Fishing as the leading product category.
